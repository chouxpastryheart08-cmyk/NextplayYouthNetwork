Our Mission

Nextplay Youth Network is a nonprofit building a community wellness and healing framework for the Chicago Southland, advancing health equity, youth development, and mentorship for the next generation.

Crown & Compass Collective

Programs built by age, purpose, and stage

Every young person deserves a structured, caring space to grow. Our three divisions meet them there.

For Girls

The Crown

Mentorship and wellness programming centered on identity, sisterhood, leadership, and confidence, built around real, lasting relationships.

Launching Fall 2026 →
For Boys

The Compass

Guided mentorship helping boys build direction, discipline, and character through structured activities and consistent adult connection.

In development →
High School

Bridge Division

Life-skills and leadership programming for high schoolers navigating the transition to adulthood, college, and career readiness.

In development →

Community Ecosystem

We build in partnership, not isolation

Nextplay is actively developing relationships across the Chicago Southland to widen access for the youth we serve.

Inclusive & Sensory-Friendly Play

In conversation with local venues to bring accessible, sensory-friendly community events to neurodivergent youth and families.

Sports & Recreation Access

Building relationships with regional golf and youth sports foundations for skills clinics and exposure opportunities.

Schools & Local Organizations

Open to co-hosting with schools, faith communities, and youth-serving nonprofits across the region.
